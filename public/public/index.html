THE FINE PRINT

House rules 📜

Keep it fair, friendly, and festive.

  1. Participants must be at least 18 years old.
  2. Participants must be members of the official TotallyTapi Discord server and sign in with that Discord account.
  3. One daily check-in per person per Eastern calendar day. No backdated check-ins.
  4. Follow the community guidelines and treat other contestants respectfully.
  5. No alternate accounts, bots, fake check-ins, point manipulation, or duplicate submissions.
  6. All event attendance, missions, and bonus points are subject to moderator verification.
  7. Social follows are optional and do not award points. No purchase or paid subscription is required.
  8. Moderators may correct scoring errors and review suspected abuse. Any disqualification must be based on the published rules.
  9. The highest verified eligible point total at the close of the contest wins. Ties will be resolved through a pre-announced Christmas trivia tiebreaker.
  10. Winner eligibility, prize model/value, delivery arrangements, eligible locations, and any applicable tax responsibilities will be stated in the final official rules before launch.

This promotion is hosted independently by TotallyTapi. Discord, Twitch, Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube do not sponsor, endorse, administer, or associate with this promotion.
